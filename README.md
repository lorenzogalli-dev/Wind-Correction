# Wind Correction

Wind Correction is an athletics-focused application that estimates how a performance would change under different wind conditions.

In track and field, wind has a significant impact on sprint and jump events, making it difficult to compare results obtained under different conditions. This app allows athletes, coaches, and analysts to normalize performances or explore hypothetical wind scenarios in a simple and reliable way.

## Download

https://apps.apple.com/it/app/wind-correction/id6738150976

## Features

- Select an athletics event (sprints or jumps)
- Input the recorded performance
- Specify the wind conditions during the performance
- Choose a target wind value (e.g. neutral wind)
- Compute the wind-corrected time or measurement

## Methodology

The correction algorithm is based on data-driven research rather than heuristic rules.

It follows the methodology described in the paper:

**Data-Driven Quantification of the Effect of Wind on Athletics Performance** (2018)

by:
- M. Moinat  
- O. Fabius  
- K. S. Emanuel  

The model was derived from large datasets of official athletics results and provides event-specific wind correction estimates.

## Disclaimer

The results provided by this application are estimates and should not be considered official or definitive. Wind correction is inherently probabilistic and is most reliable within realistic wind ranges.
